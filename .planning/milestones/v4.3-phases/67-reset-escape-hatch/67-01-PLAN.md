<objective>
Add two pure, unit-testable recovery methods to `CalcState`:

- `soft_reset(&mut self)` — clears every transient working-state field and every
  input-trapping mode/field; preserves every stored-user-data field.
- `memory_lost(&mut self)` — factory wipe, exactly `*self = CalcState::new()`.

Neither is an `Op`; neither goes through `dispatch()`. This is the single source
of truth for "what a reset clears" (no SC-4 duplication in frontends). Every
frontend (Wave 2) invokes these methods outside the key→Op dispatch path.

Purpose: give the engine the recovery primitive that the CLI/GUI/iOS escape
hatches call. Output: two methods on `CalcState` + an exhaustive test file.
</objective>
